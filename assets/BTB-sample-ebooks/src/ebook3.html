Speak Without Fear

A free 7-day plan to build speaking confidence
Spend just 15 minutes a day. Small daily practice beats long weekend sessions.

Day 1: Mirror talk

Stand in front of a mirror and introduce yourself for one minute.

Day 2: Read aloud

Read one news article aloud. Focus on clear words, not speed.

Day 3: Describe your room

Talk about five things you can see, in full sentences.

Day 4: Record yourself

Record a two-minute voice note about your day and listen once.

Day 5: Ask a question

Ask one question in English to a shopkeeper, friend or colleague.

Day 6: Think in English

For ten minutes, plan your day in English instead of your mother tongue.

Day 7: Share a story

Tell a friend a short story in English. Mistakes are welcome.
