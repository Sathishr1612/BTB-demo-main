Interview English Starter Kit

Answer common interview questions with confidence

1. Tell me about yourself

Structure: present, then past, then future. Keep it under one minute.

"I am a commerce graduate. For the last year I have worked as an accounts assistant, handling billing and GST entries. I am now looking for a role where I can grow into financial analysis."

2. What are your strengths?

"I am well organised. In my last job I created a simple tracker that reduced late payments."

3. What is your weakness?

"I used to hesitate to speak in meetings. I joined spoken English classes, and now I share at least one idea in every meeting."

4. Why should we hire you?

"I have the basic skills you need, I learn fast, and I want to grow with the company."

5. Questions to ask the interviewer

  • What does a typical day in this role look like?
  • How will my performance be measured?
  • What are the next steps?
