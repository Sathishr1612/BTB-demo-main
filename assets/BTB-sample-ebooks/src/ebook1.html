50 Daily English Phrases

Ready-to-use sentences for home, work and travel

At home

PhraseWhen to use it
Could you pass me the salt, please?Asking politely at the table
I will be back in ten minutes.Leaving for a short time
What shall we cook tonight?Planning a meal
Did you sleep well?Morning greeting
I am running late today.Telling someone you are late
Can you help me with this?Asking for help

At work

PhraseWhen to use it
Could we schedule a quick call?Setting up a meeting
I will send the report by evening.Giving a deadline
Sorry, could you repeat that?When you did not hear clearly
Let me check and get back to you.When you need time
I see your point, but...Disagreeing politely
Thank you for your support.Showing thanks

Travel and shopping

PhraseWhen to use it
How much does this cost?Asking the price
Do you have this in a bigger size?Shopping for clothes
Which platform does the train leave from?At the station
Can I pay by UPI?Paying at a shop
Is there a pharmacy nearby?Finding a place
Practice tip: pick five phrases every morning and say each one aloud three times. Use two of them in a real conversation before night.

Small talk starters

  • Have you been here before?
  • What do you do on weekends?
