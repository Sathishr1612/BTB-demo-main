About the academy

Your Goals. Our Support.

Break the Barriers Academy is an online spoken-English and communication training academy. We don’t just teach English rules. We help you use English in real life.

Through live practice, friendly guidance and personalised corrections, you work on speaking, pronunciation and everyday and professional communication, one session at a time.

Our focus: helping you speak, improve and grow.
