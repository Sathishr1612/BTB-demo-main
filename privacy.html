Who we are

Break the Barriers Academy ("we", "us") provides online spoken-English and communication training. You can reach us on WhatsApp +91 93600 46012.

Information we collect

  • Contact and booking forms: your name, your phone number or email, and the message you write.
  • Student account: your name, email address and password (stored only in encrypted form by our authentication provider).
  • Purchases: the course you buy, the amount, the order and payment reference and the payment status. Your card, UPI or bank details are entered on Razorpay's secure payment window and are not stored by us.
  • Learning activity: which course you are enrolled in. [If lesson-progress tracking is switched on, also which lessons you have completed.]

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiries and arrange classes.

  • To keep the service working and fix problems.

Who processes your data
